MAHESHMATTI MOTEL - website files
=================================
index.html  - page structure and text
style.css   - colours, fonts, layout, animations
script.js   - menus, pages, video, gallery, lightning, booking form
images/     - the pictures used on the site

HOW TO OPEN:  double-click index.html (keep all files and the images folder together).

THINGS TO CHANGE (open script.js, the top of the file):
  WA        -> your WhatsApp number with country code, no + sign (example 919876543210)
  LIVE_URL  -> paste your YouTube live / video link between the quotes
  VIDEOS    -> your other YouTube videos
Also search index.html for "9876543210", "your road, your town" and "maheshmattimotel.com" and replace with your real phone, address and email. Room prices are in index.html too.

TO PUT IT ONLINE: upload the whole folder to Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages or any web host.
